# Help and bug reports

Personal Tracker appears on your phone as **Expense Tracker**. This repository
provides its Android downloads and user information. The current APK is distributed
through GitHub, not Google Play; it is not evidence of Google's approval.

## Before reporting a problem

1. Check that you are using the latest version on the
   [Releases page](https://github.com/vedhaS18-cyber/PersonalTracker/releases/latest).
2. If you already have this app installed, install the matching update over it.
   Do not uninstall to resolve an update problem without first backing up both
   Spending and Claims.
3. For missing payments, open **Spending → ⋮ → Help & capture status → Capture
   status**. Note whether capture is connected to Android and whether the source
   app is selected. The app reads notifications, not your SMS or email inbox.

Notification capture can miss or misinterpret payments. Compare your records with
your actual payments and correct or add entries when needed. Do not use the app as
your only financial record.

## Installation messages

If Android asks whether your browser or file manager can install this app, grant
that permission only for an APK downloaded from this repository. You can turn it
off again after installation. Keep Play Protect enabled.

If Android reports an update conflict or a different signing certificate, keep the
existing app installed and report the exact error with its app version. An app
with the same visible name may have a different installation identity. Do not
uninstall or clear storage as a workaround; that can delete local records.

## Report an ordinary bug or request a feature

[Open an issue](https://github.com/vedhaS18-cyber/PersonalTracker/issues/new/choose)
with:

- App version, phone model and Android version.
- What you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.
- Steps that reproduce the issue, including whether you switched apps, locked the
  phone or restarted it.
- For capture problems, the source app name and the non-sensitive status shown in
  Capture status.

**Issues are public.** Use invented transactions and sample receipts where
possible. Remove names, email addresses, phone numbers, bank or card numbers,
transaction references, QR codes and unrelated notifications from screenshots.
Do not upload complete backups, original bank emails, passwords or signing keys.
Check images after redacting them before attaching them.

For a security weakness or suspected exposure of private information, use the
[private reporting instructions](SECURITY.md). Do not include security-sensitive
details in a public issue.

Support is maintained on a best-effort basis. There is currently no dedicated
support email or guaranteed response time. The app cannot recover records after
local data is deleted unless you have a suitable backup.

See [Privacy and data handling](PRIVACY.md) for storage, permissions and deletion
details.
