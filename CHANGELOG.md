# Release notes

## v1.5.0 — 20 September 2026

Published here on 5 October 2026, with the same APK bytes and update identity as the existing v1.5.0 release. There is no new app version or rebuild in this publication.

- Scan up to 10 receipt pages in one session, preserving their accepted order.
- Import existing gallery photos into the scanner for cropping and rotation. Direct Gallery attachment remains available.
- Choose Original or Filters before scanning. Original is the default for a new bill.
- Keep unsaved camera and scanned pages in private draft storage so clearing Android's disposable cache does not remove them.
- Identify a failing photo and its cause. Keep successfully imported pages when another page fails, and wait for import to finish before allowing Save.
- Improve scan controls at large text sizes and number receipt thumbnails.

Existing notification-capture behavior is unchanged. This release does not claim to resolve every missed Gmail or bank alert. See [verification and remaining device checks](docs/verification.md).
